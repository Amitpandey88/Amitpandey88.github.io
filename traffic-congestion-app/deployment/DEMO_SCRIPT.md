# Hackathon Demo Script

1. Open dashboard → Overview KPIs load (alerts, speed, hotspots).
2. Navigate to Prediction Panel.
3. Select location/time and simulate rain + accident.
4. Click **Run Prediction** to show 30–60 minute congestion risk and confidence.
5. Open Live Map/Heatmap → hotspot circles highlighted.
6. Open Route & Toll Comparison:
   - Compare Fastest, Lowest Toll, AI Recommended routes
   - Show ETA, toll, risk, fuel score, recommendation tags
7. Switch mode to **Emergency** and rerun route compare.
8. Open Alerts Center to show triggered advisories.
9. Ask chatbot: “Which area is likely congested in next 45 minutes?”
10. Show Preventive action recommendations and close with future scope.
