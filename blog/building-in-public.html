Why Computer & Data Science Students Should Build in Public

Published on January 10, 2026 • 4 min read • by Amit Pandey

My GitHub (@Amitpandey88) is my digital lab. Treating your repositories as open experiments rather than pristine final products accelerates learning tenfold.

When you share your code, document your technical decisions, and write writeups, you don't just learn faster — you build a visible record of your growth.
